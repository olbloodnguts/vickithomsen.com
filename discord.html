Join the Family Discord

The Vicki Thomsen Family server on Discord is a private place for Vicki’s family and friends to stay connected, share photos, plan get-togethers, and keep in touch. It’s free, and getting in takes about ten minutes.

What is Discord?

Discord is a free app for chatting with a group of people. Think of it as a family group text, only tidier: conversations are sorted by topic, so news, photos, and everyday chat each have their own place and don’t get buried.

A group on Discord is called a server. Inside a server, each topic is a channel, and channel names start with a #, like #family-chat. You can also send a private message to just one person.

Never used it before? Discord’s own Beginner’s Guide to Discord walks through the basics.

This is our family’s space

Lots of Discord servers are open to anyone. Ours isn’t. The Vicki Thomsen Family server isn’t listed anywhere public, and the invite link alone won’t get anyone in. Everyone has to send an application, and a family admin approves each one by hand. Only people we’ve approved can see or post anything there.

Before you start, you’ll need:

  • A Discord account, or an email address you can check to make one.
  • To be at least 13 years old (Discord’s rule).

You can use Discord on a phone, a tablet, or a computer. On a phone, the app is easiest.

Step 1. Sign in to Discord, or create an account

Already have a Discord account? You can use it. Sign in, check these three things, then go on to Step 2.

  • Forgot your password? On the login screen, tap Forgot your password? and Discord will email you a reset link.
  • Verified email: if Discord shows a banner asking you to verify your email, do that first. You can’t post on the family server until it’s verified.
  • Your name: if your Discord name isn’t your real name, that’s fine. You can set a name just for the family server after you join (see Step 4).

New to Discord? Create a free account:

  • On a phone or tablet: install Discord from the App Store or Google Play, open it, and tap Register.
  • On a computer: go to discord.com/register.

Discord will ask for your email, a display name, a username, a password, and your date of birth.

  • Display name is what the family sees. Please use your real name, like “Vicki Thomsen,” so everyone knows who you are.
  • Username is your unique login name. Anything is fine. Discord will tell you if it’s taken.

Discord may ask you to prove you’re not a robot. Then it sends you an email. Open it and tap Verify Email.

Step 2. Apply to join the family server

Once you’re signed in to Discord, tap the button below. On a phone, it opens the Discord app.

Join the Vicki Thomsen Family server

To keep the server family-only, Discord will show you a short application. You’ll agree to the server rules and answer a few questions, like your full name and who invited you. Then submit it.

Step 3. Wait for approval

A family admin reviews every application by hand, so it may take a little while. They might send you a quick message in Discord first if they have a question.

Once you’re approved, the server shows up in your server list on the left side of Discord.

Step 4. Say hello

You’ll start in the ✌️ Welcome section. Take a minute to read #welcome-start-here and #rules, then say hi in #family-chat.

If you joined with an account you already had and your name there isn’t your real name, set a name just for this server. Tap the server’s name at the top of your channel list, choose Edit Server Profile (some versions say Edit Per-server Profile), type your real name as your Nickname, and save. This only changes how you appear on the family server.

An admin will also give you access to your part of the family: Vicki’s children, her grandchildren, family friends, or friends of the grandkids. New sections will appear in your channel list on their own. You don’t need to do anything.

What’s inside

  • #announcements: family news and plans.
  • #family-chat: everyday conversation, photos, and catching up.
  • family: voice, video & screen share: tap it to join a call with whoever is there.
  • Private sections just for your part of the family, once you have access.

If something goes wrong

I can’t type in a channel.
Some channels, like #welcome-start-here, #rules, and #announcements, are read-only for everyone. If you can’t post anywhere, make sure you clicked Verify Email in the email Discord sent you (Step 1). Brand-new accounts also have to wait about five minutes before they can post.
I applied but haven’t heard back.
Applications are approved by hand, so give it some time. If it’s been a while, text the family member who sent you this page.
The invite link says it’s invalid or expired.
Ask the family member who sent you this page for a fresh link.
Discord asked me to confirm my age.
Discord now checks ages across its whole service. Nothing on the family server is age-restricted, so you can skip it and still use the server.
I’m getting too many notifications.
Press and hold (or right-click) the server’s icon on the left, open Notification Settings, and choose Only @mentions.
I’m stuck.
Reach out to the family member who sent you this page. We’re happy to help.

We’re glad you’re here.
